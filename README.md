# Asheville Book Club

Official website of the **Asheville Book Club** in Asheville, North Carolina. The club connects authors with active readers through live Zoom sessions, recorded video messages and creative features, and celebrates a featured book every month.

Organized by **Tobias Roth**.

## What is on the website

- **Home:** the club's story, how authors take part, benefits, the club pick and the featured authors
- **2026 featured season:** a themed page for each month, with a signature event idea and a place for reader reviews
- **Reading archive:** a searchable list of featured books and authors, filtered by genre
- **Author features and participation:** author profiles and a clear explanation of how to take part
- **Events and book discussions:** an events calendar and spoiler-aware discussion questions
- **Forms:** author submission, book recommendation, contact, reader reviews and newsletter signup
- **Author reviews panel:** video reviews from featured authors
- **Privacy policy and terms of use**

## How the site works

The whole website is one file, `index.html`, with its images, audio and video built in. There is nothing to install and no build step. It runs on GitHub Pages or any ordinary web host.

All forms open the visitor's email app with a ready-made message addressed to the club. The site does not store visitors' details.

## Updating the site

1. Open `index.html` in this repository and choose **Edit** (the pencil icon), or upload a new copy with **Add file > Upload files**.
2. Commit the change. GitHub Pages updates the live site within a minute or two.

Things the club will update regularly: the book of the month, new featured authors, events and approved reader reviews.
